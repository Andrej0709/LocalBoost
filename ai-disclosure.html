AI DISCLOSURE

Every creative is AI-generated. Every creative is human-approved.

Here's exactly how the engine works, and the guardrails that stop it from shipping something wrong.

How creatives are made

Each weekly drop is rendered by generative image and video models, conditioned on the brand fingerprint you set during onboarding — your palette, your props, your storefront, your tone. Nothing is stock footage and nothing is a template with your logo pasted on top; every scene is generated fresh for your business.

What "human-approved" means

No AI-rendered creative reaches your channels without a human clicking approve. That human is you — you approve or kill every creative in the drop from the control room before it ever publishes. LocalBoost never auto-publishes anything you haven't seen.

Realism guardrails

Before a creative reaches your approval queue, it passes through automated checks built to catch the failure modes generative models are known for:

  • No invented prices, offers, or claims that weren't in your brief.
  • No distorted hands, faces, or storefront signage — anything below a confidence threshold is discarded, not shipped.
  • No fabricated reviews, testimonials, or star ratings.
  • No competitor names, logos, or trademarks.

Creatives that fail a guardrail never enter your queue — they're regenerated automatically before the drop is finalized.

Your customers and AI content

Where a platform or local regulation requires disclosure that an ad contains AI-generated imagery, LocalBoost applies the required label automatically at publish time. You're never responsible for tracking which rules apply where.

Questions

If a specific creative looks off, you can flag it from the control room, or reach us through our contact page.
