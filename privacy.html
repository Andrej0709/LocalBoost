Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026. What we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: business name, owner name, email, city, vertical.
  • Brand fingerprint assets: reference photos, palette, tone notes you upload during onboarding.
  • Channel tokens: the access tokens needed to publish to connected platforms.
  • Usage data: which creatives you approve or kill, and engagement metrics pulled back from connected channels.

2. How we use it

We use your brand fingerprint and usage data to render creatives that look like your business and to tune future drops toward what performs. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your brand assets to train models for other customers.

3. Third-party channels

When you connect Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google Business, LinkedIn or Pinterest, we exchange data with those platforms only to publish your approved creatives and to pull back performance metrics. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle that data on their end.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and a small number of analytics cookies to understand which parts of the product get used. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser without losing core functionality.

5. Data retention

Brand fingerprint assets and rendered creatives are kept for as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after cancellation in case you want to reactivate. You can request full deletion sooner from the control room or by contacting us.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you're based, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Reach out through our contact page and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Channel tokens and account data are encrypted at rest and in transit. Access to production data is limited to the engineers who operate the rendering and publishing pipeline.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are emailed to active accounts at least 14 days before they take effect.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests go to our contact page.
